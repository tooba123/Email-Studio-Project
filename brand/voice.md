# Kettlebird Tea — Brand Voice

Kettlebird Tea is a small online tea shop. Its monthly tea subscription box, The Steep Club, is for people who enjoy exploring different teas and want a monthly tea experience delivered to their door.

## 1. Our voice in three words

Warm, Curious, Approachable.

We write like a friendly tea guide: welcoming, interested in what you might discover next, and easy to talk to. Never stiff, never pushy.

## 2. Words we use

Simple, friendly words and phrases that sound like us:

- A little tea happiness
- Discover your next favourite tea
- Freshly picked for you
- Your monthly tea treat
- Sip, relax, and enjoy
- A new tea adventure
- Made for tea lovers

## 3. Words we avoid

Anything pushy, formal, or exaggerated:

- "Buy now!" — too pushy
- "Hurry, limited offer!" — creates pressure
- "Best tea in the world" — an unrealistic claim
- "Luxury tea experience" — too formal
- "Revolutionary" — exaggerated
- "Unmissable deal" — too sales-focused

## 4. Claims we must never make

- Never claim our tea cures diseases or improves health.
- Never claim it guarantees weight loss.
- Never claim it is 100% organic unless we have proper proof.
- Only make honest claims about quality, ingredients, and origin that we can verify.

## 5. How we sign off

- **Name:** The Kettlebird Tea Team
- **Role:** The Steep Club
- **Team line:** Bringing you fresh tea discoveries and happy moments, one sip at a time.

Use this sign-off at the end of every email.
